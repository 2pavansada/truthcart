1. Returns and Refunds
1.1 Return Window
Customers may return eligible items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and with all tags intact.

1.2 Refund Processing
Refunds will be credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes quality inspection.

1.3 Return Shipping Fee
Return shipping is free for damaged or defective items; otherwise a return shipping fee of ₹100 applies.

2. Shipping and Delivery
2.1 Delivery Timelines
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days across major metro cities in India. Remote locations may take up to 7 business days.

2.2 Express Shipping
Express shipping delivers within 1 to 2 business days for an additional fee of ₹150.

2.3 Order Cancellation
Orders can only be cancelled within 2 hours of placement before dispatch. Once dispatched, orders cannot be cancelled and must be returned after delivery.

3. Warranty and Guarantees
3.1 General Store Warranty
Unless a manufacturer warranty is specified on the product, TruthCart provides a 6-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

3.2 Warranty Exclusions
Normal wear and tear, physical damage, accidental drops, and liquid immersion are excluded from warranty coverage.
